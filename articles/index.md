# Articles

### Getting and understanding Vannmiljø data

How to get data out of Vannmiljø and what the columns in an export mean.

- [Getting data from
  Vanmniljø](https://sawelch-niva.github.io/Vm2eData/articles/vannmiljo-getting-data.md):

  How we get data from Vannmiljø. So far only covers the frontend.

- [Variables in the Oslo January 2020
  export](https://sawelch-niva.github.io/Vm2eData/articles/vannmiljo-data-variables.md):

  A walk through the columns of a raw Vannmiljø water-registration
  export, what each one means, and what we do with it on the way to
  eData.

- [Variables in the Oslo sites
  export](https://sawelch-niva.github.io/Vm2eData/articles/vannmiljo-site-variables.md):

  A walk through the columns of a raw Vannmiljø water-location (site)
  export, what each one means, and what we do with it on the way to
  eData.

### Converting to eData

How Vannmiljø records are turned into eData compartments, geographic

- [Converting sites and
  compartments](https://sawelch-niva.github.io/Vm2eData/articles/vannmiljo-site-compartment-conversion.md):

  How we turn Vannmiljø’s site categories and sample media into eData
